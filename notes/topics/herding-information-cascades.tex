\chapter{Herding and Information Cascades}

\begin{itemize}
    \item People change their behavior based on what others are doing
    \item Information cascade: people make same decisions as others regardless of private information
    \item Subjects use conditional probability to make the best decisions based on others' behaviors
    \item Bayes' Rule:
          \begin{equation}
              \prob(A \given B) = \frac{\prob(A) \prob(B \given A)}{\prob(B)}
          \end{equation}
\end{itemize}
